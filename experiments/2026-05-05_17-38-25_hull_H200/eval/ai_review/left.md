# Left logo — review

The left-panel composite reads as a real painted ad. The Red Bull bull-and-wordmark sits naturally on the blue panel; the diagonal seam line at the panel join is preserved, which is the dead giveaway when a paste-on misses. Across early, mid and late strips the logo holds at the same scale and rake — no walking, no wobble. Color and brightness track the broadcast's slight cool cast. Indistinguishable from the YoPRO original.
