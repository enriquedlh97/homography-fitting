# back — short prose

A scrubbing viewer would read the back banner Red Bull as a real painted-on
sponsor: no halo at the perimeter, no luminance bloom, and the wordmark sits
on the dark banner fabric with crisp letter edges. The bull silhouettes show
only a minimal edge-reflex if scrutinised at full zoom — well within the
quality bar set by the original Kia mark. Brightness is a hair high on the
white wordmark relative to the slightly subdued Kia original (4/5 not 5/5).
Across early/mid/late motion strips the mark holds steady with no swim, no
flicker, no rectangle seam. This region is essentially solved at the rubric-v2
calibration bar.
