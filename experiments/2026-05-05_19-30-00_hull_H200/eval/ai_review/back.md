# back — visual review

A scrubbing viewer would read the Red Bull replacement on the back LED banner as a legitimate painted-on ad. With alpha_feather=2 and mask_dilate=8, the patch has crisp letter edges that match the original Kia bake quality — no halo glow at the wordmark perimeter, no rectangular boundary seam. The dark navy banner provides strong contrast which the tight feather handles well, since the LED fixture frame above clearly delimits the surface. Across early/mid/late strips there is no perceptible jitter and no perimeter pop at any scrub position. Slight letter-edge softness on bull rings under motion blur is comparable to the original. Brightness and saturation track the underlying banner tone naturally. Verdict: parity with P3-A5 baseline on back banners — the very tight feather did not regress this region.
