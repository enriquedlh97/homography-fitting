A viewer scrubbing this region would immediately notice the Red Bull logo has lost almost all its contrast — the wordmark and bulls icon dissolve into the blue diagonal banner like a half-erased decal. Compared against the original YoPRO baked-in ad above, which is crisp opaque white, the composite reads as semi-transparent and underexposed. The local-color-match step on obj_4 has pulled the logo too aggressively toward the banner's mean color, and the lum_strength=0.3 darkens it further. Ironically the edge_reflex and halo dimensions both score 5 here — but only because the logo has been bleached down to where there are no edges left to ring or glow. This is a failure mode that the v2 calibration was not designed to catch: the rubric improvements on edge_reflex came at the cost of the logo's basic legibility.
