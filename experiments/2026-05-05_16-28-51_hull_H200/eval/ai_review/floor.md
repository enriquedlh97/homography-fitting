# floor — viewer scrub notes

Floor-logo region is the load-bearing surface for this run, and it holds up. Through early, mid, and late motion strips the Red Bull floor mark stays planted on the court — no breathing, no edge slither against the court paint lines, no halo where the mark meets the surrounding blue. Brightness and saturation track the Melbourne wordmark original; the white reads as printed-on court vinyl, not pasted. Perspective foreshortening is consistent across the strip's many frames — the same horizontal compression the original suffers from is reproduced cleanly. No player crosses the logo in these strips (that's the walkover region), so occlusion isn't tested here, but the static-stability bar is fully met. A viewer would read this as a real court sponsor.
