# floor — visual review

The floor logo is the canonical halo case. The baked-in MELBOURNE wordmark on the original sits flat and matte, absorbed into the court paint; our Red Bull composite reads luminous against that same matte surface — the white type and bull silhouettes emit a faint glow that the original does not. A scrubbing viewer would clock the logo as a sticker rather than paint within a few seconds. Hue and saturation track the court blue well; brightness is too high. Geometry on the perspective plane is solid and stable, no jitter. Critically, occlusion fidelity over the player feet is weak: when the player traverses the wordmark in the walkover window, the suspected-leak overlay shows the original MELBOURNE letters bleeding through under the shoes. The dilation knob did not visibly clean this up vs. the P3-A5 baseline.
