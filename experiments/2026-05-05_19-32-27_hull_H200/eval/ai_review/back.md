# back — visual review

The back banner sits deep in shadow behind the baseline. The Kia original (top row) is dim and slightly purple-tinted by the venue lighting; the Red Bull composite (bottom row) reads a touch brighter and whiter than its dim neighbours. A scrubbing viewer would mainly notice that lift in brightness, not a seam or a halo — the banner fabric is dark and uniform, so the calibration artifacts (halo, edge reflex) do not show up here. Perspective and size are correct; the wordmark sits within the existing banner band cleanly. There is no obvious shimmer or jitter across the early/mid/late motion strips; the composite is temporally stable. Net: passes as a painted-in ad, just one notch shy of indistinguishable because of the brightness mismatch with the dim background.
