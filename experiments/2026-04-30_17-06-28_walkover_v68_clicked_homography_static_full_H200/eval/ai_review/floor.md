# floor — viewer notes

Scrubbing the floor region, a viewer sees a Red Bull bull-and-wordmark painted on the court where the original "MELBOURNE" wordmark lived. Geometry is the strong point: the logo lies flat on the court plane, scale is right, and there is no visible jitter or swimming as the camera pans through the early/mid/late strips. Color is close — the blue around the logo matches the court paint convincingly. The main tell is that the composite reads a touch cleaner and more uniform than a real painted court mark, which would have more brightness variation, scuffs and specular response. Player-shadow interaction is the weakest dimension: when feet pass nearby the logo doesn't pick up the localized shading you would expect from real paint under a player's contact shadow. Overall it sits comfortably below the rear/left banners but is still a credible insertion when not under direct walkover.
