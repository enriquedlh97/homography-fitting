The left side banner is the cleanest region on color and brightness — the deep Red Bull blue sits naturally against the surrounding panel paint, and you don't see the brightness step that dogs the back row. The flaw here is sizing: Red Bull's wordmark and bull silhouette occupy a smaller share of the diagonal panel than the YoPRO original they replaced, leaving visible blue padding above and below the logo. Sister panels in the same frame use the full height, so the eye picks up the inconsistency immediately. There's also a faint reflex along letter edges and the bull ring outlines — subtle but present when compared against YoPRO's crisp strokes in the top row. Geometry holds across motion strips with no jitter. Net: a viewer probably wouldn't call out paint-on quality, but they would notice "that one panel is smaller."
