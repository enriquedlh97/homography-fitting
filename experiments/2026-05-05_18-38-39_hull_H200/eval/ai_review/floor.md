# floor

The floor courtside panel originally carries the MELBOURNE wordmark in white type and the composite swaps in Red Bull. Geometry and perspective are excellent — the logo sits flush with the panel across all three motion strips and there is no visible breathing or jitter. The honest failure mode is the halo: there is a soft bright glow around the bulls and the Red Bull letters on the matte blue panel, which the original MELBOURNE wordmark does not have. Against that baseline this is a 2, not a 5 — exactly the user-flagged calibration case. Letter edges show a mild reflex but are otherwise clean. Late frames (~626–656 in motion_strip_late) reveal a very slight camera drift on the panel, but the logo remains locked. Player contact moments (see walkover region) hand off occlusion well, but the halo is the dominant artifact a viewer would notice when scrubbing.
