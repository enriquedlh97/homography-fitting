# back

A scrubbing viewer would see Red Bull cleanly substituted for the original Kia wordmark on the back banner. The hue, brightness and saturation of the fabric are preserved on either side of the swap, and the diagonal banner perspective tracks the geometry naturally as the camera moves between early, mid and late strips. There is no bright halo on the matte banner around the bulls or letters; the swap reads painted-on rather than pasted-on. The bull horns and rings show only a very faint hint of edge softness compared with the crisper Kia "K" in the originals — not a true reflex/ghost, more a small drop in ink contrast. Stability is excellent — the logo does not breathe between frames, consistent with the BTN ~3 px frame-to-frame stability claim. Overall this region is at near-original quality.
