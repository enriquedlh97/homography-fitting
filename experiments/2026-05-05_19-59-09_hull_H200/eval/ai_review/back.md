# back — what a viewer would notice

The Kia → Red Bull replacement on the back banner reads as a clean static print on the dark backstop. Across early, mid, and late motion strips the patch is locked in place — no perimeter rectangle, no walking edge, no luminance halo. The dark surrounding panel hides most micro-edge artifacts, so the casual viewer would not flag this region. On close inspection a faint softness lingers on the lower edge of the bull silhouettes and around the wordmark — the original Kia baked-in ad is crisper than our Red Bull insertion, so `edge_reflex` is 4 not 5. Hue and brightness sit a hair cool relative to the panel but well within "real ad print" territory. With BTN's stable H and the tighter `mask_dilate=4`, the patch is snug; nothing about this region pops against the broadcast.
