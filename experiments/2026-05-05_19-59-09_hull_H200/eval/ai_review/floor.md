# floor — what a viewer would notice

The synthesis test of the P3-A6 sweep: alpha_feather=4 + mask_dilate=4 on top of BTN's stable H. A viewer scrubbing this region would not see a halo around the Red Bull wordmark — the canonical Melbourne floor glow is gone. They also would not see the hard rectangular step that P3-A6/a2 (feather=2, dilate=8) introduced: with feather=4 the patch edge has enough falloff to blend into the surrounding paint, and the matched dilate=4 means the feather is no longer fighting an over-trimmed mask. A very faint perimeter line is still detectable on the top edge in mid and late strips during slow scrub, so this dimension is 4 rather than P3-A5's 5. No jitter; the patch is locked frame-to-frame. Player contact shadow is the only properly weak axis — the mark stays opaque under feet rather than darkening under contact — but that is a separate experiment axis from the feather/dilate sweep.
