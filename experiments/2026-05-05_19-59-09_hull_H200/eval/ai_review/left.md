# left — what a viewer would notice

The YoPRO → Red Bull swap on the left side-banner is the cleanest region of the run. The Red Bull wordmark and bulls sit flat against the uniform blue panel; no rectangular perimeter seam is visible at any motion-strip position, and there is no luminance halo at the logo border. With `alpha_feather=4` the letter edges are crisp without ringing, and `mask_dilate=4` trims the patch snug to the bake. Hue match against the original baked YoPRO ad is excellent — same panel-blue value, same saturation register. The remaining weakness is a faint softness at the top edge of the wordmark and around the bulls' wing-tips: the YoPRO bake is slightly crisper than our Red Bull insertion. That residual softness is interior detail, not a perimeter artifact, so the tighter dilate did not move this dimension off 4. No jitter; no walking patch edge.
